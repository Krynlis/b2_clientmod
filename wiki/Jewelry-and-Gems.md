# Jewelry and gems

The extended inventory has slots for **1 necklace, 2 rings, 1 belt and 4 gems**. Each slot only accepts matching items. Jewelry is made by the Lunarei, higher gem tiers by the Mirna (see [Peoples](Peoples)).

- **Necklaces** are triggered by you: sneak + left-click into the air, then a cooldown follows.
- **Rings** work when you hit or kill, **belts** when you are hit or mine, each with a chance.
- **Gems** give fixed stats. There are 10 kinds with 10 tiers each: Shard, Rough, Cloudy, Cut, Clear, Pure, Flawless, Radiant, Royal, Celestial.
- **Rarity:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
