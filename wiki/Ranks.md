# Ranks

Your **rank** is shown in square brackets before your name, e.g. "[Ritterin] Name". There are 10 ranks, each with a male and a female form. You choose the form once after choosing your people; only an admin can change it. Ranks give no special permissions.

| Tier | Male | Female |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neuling | Neuling |
| 2 | Knecht | Magd |
| 3 | Knappe | Knappin |
| 4 | Bürger | Bürgerin |
| 5 | Geselle | Gesellin |
| 6 | Meister | Meisterin |
| 7 | Ritter | Ritterin |
| 8 | Vogt | Vögtin |
| 9 | Burgherr | Burgherrin |
| 10 | Graf | Gräfin |

**Team:** The team roles [Supporter], [Dev] and [Eigentümer] (owner) are shown after the name. Ask them if you need help.

Your people is not shown at your name but as a heading in the tab list.
