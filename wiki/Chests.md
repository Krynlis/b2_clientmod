# Chests

Chests, trapped chests, copper chests, barrels, shulker boxes and ender chests start as usual (3 rows, double chest 6). With the **upgrade orb** you enlarge them row by row: hold the orb and use it on the chest. Up to 6 rows the window grows; beyond that you scroll with the bar on the right. Chests, barrels and shulker boxes go up to 20 rows, the ender chest up to 6.

Shulker boxes keep their size and contents when broken. Hoppers have their own menu too. Only one player can open a chest at a time.
