# Discord

1. Type `/link` in game. You get a six-digit code (click to copy).
2. Type `/link <code>` on the Blame2 Discord.

Afterwards your Discord nickname is set to your Minecraft name and you automatically get the roles for your people and rank. `/unlink` removes the link. Depending on the settings, only linked players may join the server; your code is then shown right in the message when you connect.
