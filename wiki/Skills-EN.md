# Skills

There are 14 skills in four categories. They all level up as you play and their bonuses are always active. `/skills` opens the skill menu with level, XP and bonuses.

| Category | Skills | XP for | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mining | Aderkunde, Steinkenntnis, Urgestein, Höllenstein, Kristallkunde, Schaufelkunst, Sand & Kies | mined blocks | faster mining, chance of double drops |
| Wood | Waldkunde, Edelholz, Nether-Stämme, Pilzkunde, Bambus & Rohr | chopped blocks | faster chopping, chance of double drops |
| Travel | Reisen & Überleben | distance travelled (elytra counts more, damage resets progress) | less fall damage, faster walking |
| Fishing | Angeln | every catch, heavier gives more | bigger and rarer fish, wider green zone |

Blocks you placed yourself give no XP. A boss bar appears when you gain XP. Big level-ups are announced to everyone.

**Skill gems:** Each skill has gems with 10 tiers. In a gem slot of your inventory they speed up levelling that skill. Higher tiers are made by the Mirna people.
