# Travel

The menu button in the extended inventory (key E) leads to **Homes**, **Spawn** and **Warps**. `/warps` opens the warp list directly.

- **Create a home:** click "New" in the home list and enter a name.
- **Travel:** left-click a home or warp.
- **Edit:** right-click opens a submenu to rename, delete or (for homes) make it global. Global homes show up for everyone in the warp list, with your head as the icon.
- **Search warps:** the warp list has a search.
- **Spawn:** the spawn is the flying city. If you die without a bed or respawn anchor, you respawn there.
