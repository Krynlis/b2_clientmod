# Peoples

Every people has its own workstation with exclusive recipes that only this people can use. After choosing your people you get your workstation once for free; more can be crafted with a recipe. The workstations look like normal blocks; ordinary composters, enchanting tables etc. stay normal.

| People | Workstation | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Humans (Menschen) | Bait box (composter) | 4 chambers that produce all 20 baits for [fishing](Fishing). Live baits breed from a starter with food, even while nobody looks inside. |
| Lunarei | Jewelry bench (fletching table) | Crafts all 40 rings, belts and necklaces. Costs ingredients and experience levels, from rare upwards often with a gem. |
| Dirasau | Rune altar (enchanting table) | 10 custom weapon enchantments with 3 levels each, for many experience levels. |
| Mirna | Gem forge (blast furnace) | 5x5 grid with fuel and time: two gems of one tier plus ingredients make a gem of the next tier (up to 10). Mirna find tier 1 gems when mining ore. |

The bait box and gem forge keep their contents inside the block. Breaking them returns the workstation and its contents.

`/volk` opens the people choice or shows the peoples, `/volk info` shows yours.

## The peoples

- **Humans (Menschen):** versatile settlers, masters of bait breeding.
- **Lunarei:** an elven people, skilled jewelers.
- **Dirasau:** lizard folk, keepers of ancient runes.
- **Mirna:** a mountain people of dwarves and gnomes who find gems while mining.

## Crafting your workstation (normal crafting table, own people only)

| Workstation | Pattern | Ingredients |
|---|---|---|
| Bait box | `PWP / DCD / PPP` | P = planks, W = wheat, D = dirt, C = composter |
| Jewelry bench | `AGA / QFQ / PPP` | A = amethyst shard, G = gold ingot, Q = quartz, F = fletching table, P = planks |
| Rune altar | `BDB / OTO / OCO` | B = book, D = diamond, O = obsidian, T = enchanting table, C = crying obsidian |
| Gem forge | `IGI / DBD / SSS` | I = iron block, G = gold ingot, D = diamond, B = blast furnace, S = polished deepslate |

Put a weapon into the rune altar. A weapon can hold at most 3 different runes. Bow, crossbow and trident pass their runes on to the projectile. The effect works for whoever wields the weapon.

## Gems of the Mirna

Mirna sometimes find a tier 1 gem when mining ore (self-placed ore and Silk Touch don't count, deepslate ores count normally).

| Ore | Chance | Gem |
|---|---|---|
| Coal | 1 % | Onyx |
| Copper | 1.5 % | Amber |
| Iron | 2 % | Topaz, Onyx |
| Gold | 3 % | Topaz, Amber |
| Redstone | 2.5 % | Ruby |
| Lapis lazuli | 3 % | Sapphire |
| Diamond | 12 % | Diamond, Sapphire, Ruby |
| Emerald | 12 % | Emerald |
| Nether quartz | 1.5 % | Moonstone |

In the gem forge, two gems of one tier plus ingredients and fuel (coal, charcoal, blaze rod, coal block, magma block) become one gem of the next tier. Tier 10 needs 512 tier 1 gems. All gems are listed under [Jewelry and gems](Jewelry-and-Gems).
