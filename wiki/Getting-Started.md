# Getting started

1. **Install the client mod.** The server requires the client mod **b2_clientmod** (Fabric, Quilt, NeoForge or Forge). Without it you are kicked with a download link when joining.
2. **Choose your people.** On your first join you pick your people: Humans, Lunarei, Dirasau or Mirna. Then you choose whether your rank names are male or female (e.g. Burgherr or Burgherrin). Both choices are final; only an admin can change them. More under [Peoples](Peoples).
3. **Extended inventory.** Your inventory has extra slots for jewelry (necklace, rings, belt), gems and the shield hand, see [Jewelry and gems](Jewelry-and-Gems). The menu button in the inventory (or `/menu`) leads to homes, warps, spawn and skills.
4. **Tab list and sidebar.** The tab list shows all players sorted by people, plus time, date, world and the next restart. Toggle the sidebar with `/sidebar`.

**Restarts:** daily at 02:00, 10:00 and 18:00.

**Ranks:** Your rank is shown in square brackets before your name (from Neuling to Graf). Team members (Supporter, Dev, Owner) are shown after the name. More under [Ranks](Ranks).
